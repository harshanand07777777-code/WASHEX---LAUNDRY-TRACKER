Washex Policies & Campus Guidelines

This page provides general information for using the Washex campus laundry service. For official campus rules or account-specific questions, contact the campus support desk.

Washex Terms of Service

  • Use your own account details when registering or signing in.
  • Provide accurate contact and laundry-order information when using the service.
  • Keep your tracking details and any locker pickup code private.
  • Use the tracking and contact features only for legitimate laundry-service requests.
  • Contact the campus support desk if you notice an incorrect order status or account issue.

Campus Laundry Guidelines

  • Check garment care labels before submitting items for washing or treatment.
  • Remove personal belongings from pockets before handing over garments.
  • Retain your order or tracking reference so you can check the order progress.
  • Collect completed laundry using the instructions provided by the campus laundry desk.
  • Report damaged machines, missing items, or locker-access issues to campus support.

Need help? Contact the Washex support desk.
