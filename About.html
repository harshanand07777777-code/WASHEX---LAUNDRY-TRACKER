ABOUT WASHEX

Laundry that fits around campus life

Washex is a campus laundry service built for students. Drop your clothes off, follow every step online, and collect them from your locker when they are ready.

OUR STORY

No more guessing when your clothes will be done

Washing machines in shared halls are often busy, and nobody likes carrying a bag across campus to find out the laundry is not ready. Washex takes care of the washing, drying and ironing, and shows you exactly where your order is at any moment.

Every order gets a laundry number such as WX-10025. Enter it on the tracking page to see its current step, the expected collection time and the locker where it will be waiting.
